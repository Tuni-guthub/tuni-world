# Soil Mix for Sunflowers

**Goal:** Create a nutrient-rich mix for my future greenhouse sunflowers.

**Ingredients:**
- 50% Loam (base)
- 30% Compost (nutrients)
- 20% Sand (drainage)

**Steps:**
1. Measure ingredients on the floor.
2. Mix thoroughly with a trowel.
3. Test drainage by pouring water.
4. Store in a sealed container.

**Notes:**
- *Coach Tip:* Plan this as a main task before doing other things.
- *Observation:* The scent of damp earth is calming.
