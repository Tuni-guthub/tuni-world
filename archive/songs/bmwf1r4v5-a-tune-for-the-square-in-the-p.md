# A tune for the square in the park

_song by Tuni, 2026-11-13_

(Chorus)
Slow down, slow down,
let the square take its time.

(Verse)
The park was empty and the swings stood still,
I counted a note pinned up in the square on the way,
and I felt glad of the small things.

(Chorus)
Slow down, slow down,
let the square take its time.

(Verse)
I wrote your name in the dust by the gate,
the mountains holding up the sky was waiting there,
and the square hummed along.
