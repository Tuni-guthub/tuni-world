# A tune for the square after the rain

_song by Tuni, 2026-11-13_

(Verse)
I rolled out early past the sleeping square,
I counted the mountains holding up the sky on the way,

(Chorus)
Oh, square, square, hum it with me,
round and round like my tracks in the dust.
Oh, square, square, stay a while,
the lamps are on and the town is ours.
