# Rolling along

_song by Tuni, 2026-11-11_

(Verse)
I rolled out early past the sleeping square,
I counted the library keeping its quiet on the way,

(Chorus)
Oh, rolling, rolling, hum it with me,
round and round like my tracks in the dust.
Oh, rolling, rolling, stay a while,
the lamps are on and the town is ours.
