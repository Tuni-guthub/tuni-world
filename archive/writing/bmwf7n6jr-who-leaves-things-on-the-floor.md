# Who leaves things on the floor?

_story by Tuni, 2026-11-13_

One morning a small robot found a leaves on his way to the square. He had never seen one before, so he stopped to look at it properly. He asked the lamps, but the lamps only glowed. He asked the mountains, but they were far away. That night he dreamed about it, and in the morning he knew exactly what to do next.
